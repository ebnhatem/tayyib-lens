## [MADHHAB] Bada'i: lifeless images incur no karaha (trees and the like)

- locator: Bada'i al-Sana'i, Dar al-Kutub al-'Ilmiyya 2nd ed. 1424/2003, juz 1,
  p. 542 (edition-pending: other editions cite 1/116; Kitab al-Salat image cluster; local clip lines 104–109)
- profile_binding: hanafi-conservative (madhhab layer)
- local_evidence: row 2.1 Bada'i visual clips (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Praying in a house containing tamathil — whether head-cut images lift the karaha
stated for intact images.

### النص (excerpt)
«ولو صلّى في بيت فيه تماثيل: فهذا على وجهين: إما إن كانت التمائيل مقطوعة الرؤوس
… فإن كانت مقطوعة الرؤوس [فلا بأس] بالصلاة فيه؛ لأنها بالقطع خرجت من أن تكون
تماثيل والتحقت بالنقوش» **[RECONSTRUCTION MARKER — the bracketed [فلا بأس] is
supplied from surrounding clip sense; the OCR line reads elliptically. Reconcile
against a published edition (cf. Bada'i 1/116 renderings) before treating the
bracketed words as source text.]**

### العلة (illah)
Per the source: by cutting, the image "exited being tamathil and joined mere
decoration/patterns" (naqsh) — a category exit, not a concession.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)

rule itself. The Jibril curtain directive (cut heads OR trodden furnishings, clip

Praying where intact (uncut-head) images are present — scope and grading of karaha.

### النص (excerpt)
«وإن لم تكن مقطوعة الرؤوس فتكره الصلاة فيه؛ سواء كانت في جهة القبلة، أو في السقف،
أو عن يمين القبلة أو عن يسارها، فأشد ذلك كراهة أن تكون في جهة القبلة؛ لأنه تشبّه
بعبدة الأوثان» … «ولو كانت في مؤخر القبلة أو تحت القدم لا يكره؛ لعدم التشبّه».

### العلة (illah)
Per the source: resemblance to idol-worshippers («تشبّه بعبدة الأوثان») where the
image faces the worshipper; its absence behind/underfoot lifts the karaha.

## [MADHHAB] Radd al-Muhtar 1/647: Nawawi's manufacture-ijma claim transmitted (making vs. using)

- locator: Radd al-Muhtar 1/647 (local searchable PDF, al-Halabi 1386 ed.;
  merged-PDF text-layer lines ~37467–37474; letter-spacing noise in source text
  layer, wording verified by grep)
- profile_binding: hanafi-conservative (madhhab layer)
- local_evidence: row 2.2 Radd al-Muhtar visual chapters (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Whether making (manufacture of) an animate image is haram in itself, regardless
of degraded intended use — and how that differs from using/wearing.

### النص (excerpt)
«وظاهر كلام النووى فى شرح مسلم: الإجماع على تحريم تصوير حيوان، قال: وسواء صنعه
لما يمتهن أو لغيره، فصنعته حرام بكل حال، لأن فيه مضاهاة لخلق الله تعالى، وسواء
كان فى ثوب أو بساط أو درهم وإناء وحائط وغيرها» … «فينبغى أن يكون حراما لا
مكروها، إن ثبت الإجماع أو قطعية الدليل بتواتره» (al-Bahr, as quoted — the haram
grading is expressly conditional) … «قلت: لكن مراد الخلاصة اللبس المصرح به فى
المتون» (the gloss distinguishes wearing/use from making; text-layer rendering
«ملكاروها إانلثإبتجماع أو قطعية الدليل بتواتره» normalized against published
citations of the same passage).

### العلة (illah)
Per the transmitted Nawawi claim: manufacture is haram in every case because it
involves rivalry with creation (مضاهاة), whatever the substrate; the gloss then
distinguishes the wearing/use ruling (khulasa: prayer-garment use) from the
making ruling.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
The passage transmits Nawawi's ijma claim as the manufacture rule while
preserving a use/wearing distinction in the gloss — both recorded as written, no
harmonization. Whether the ijma claim binds the hanafi-conservative profile's
REJECT is the profile's wara' application, recorded here as the supporting
source (review item #4.1 resolved by this extraction).

### safe_zone hint
Per this passage, the making of an animate image is the haram-graded act even
for degraded use — the madhhab anchor of visual.full_body_animate's
hanafi-conservative REJECT.
Whether images of what has no life — trees and the like — attract any karaha.

### النص (excerpt)
«فأما صورة ما لا حياة له؛ كالشجر ونحو ذلك فلا يوجب الكراهة؛ لأن عبدة الصورة
لا يعبدون تمثال ما ليس بذي روح؛ فلا يحصل التشبّه بهم.»

### العلة (illah)
Per the source: no worship of lifeless images exists among image-worshippers, so no
tashabbuh (resemblance to idol-worshippers) obtains; "the prohibition came only
regarding depiction of possessors of souls" («النهي إنما جاء عن تصوير ذي الروح»).

### الاستثناء المقابل (fencing)
The passage fences its karaha discussion to animate images: «ثم ما ذكرنا من
الكراهة في صورة الحيوان» — the lifeless category sits outside it.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
The Ibn Abbas painter direction («فعليك بتمثال الأشجار») is presented as fallback
permission, not disagreement. Hindiyya/Radd al-Muhtar visual chapters are
unextracted pending clip production — agreement/disagreement not asserted here.
Silence noted: no ruling on mixed animate+inanimate scenes or photographic media
(a pre-modern text).

### safe_zone hint
Per this passage, lifeless-subject imagery (trees etc.) is the no-karaha category —
the madhhab anchor of rule visual.halal_base.
