# Hanafi Visual

## [MADHHAB] Bada'i: lifeless images incur no karaha (trees and the like)

- locator: Bada'i al-Sana'i, Dar al-Kutub al-'Ilmiyya 2nd ed. 1424/2003, juz 1,
  p. 542 (edition-pending: other editions cite 1/116; Kitab al-Salat image cluster; local clip lines 104–109)
- profile_binding: hanafi-conservative (madhhab layer)
- local_evidence: row 2.1 Bada'i visual clips (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Whether images of what has no life — trees and the like — attract any karaha.

### النص (excerpt)
«فأما صورة ما لا حياة له؛ كالشجر ونحو ذلك فلا يوجب الكراهة؛ لأن عبدة الصورة
لا يعبدون تمثال ما ليس بذي روح؛ فلا يحصل التشبّه بهم.»

### العلة (illah)
Per the source: no worship of lifeless images exists among image-worshippers, so no
tashabbuh (resemblance to idol-worshippers) obtains; "the prohibition came only
regarding depiction of possessors of souls" («النهي إنما جاء عن تصوير ذي الروح»).

### الاستثناء المقابل (fencing)
The passage fences its karaha discussion to animate images: «ثم ما ذكرنا من
الكراهة في صورة الحيوان» — the lifeless category sits outside it.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
The Ibn Abbas painter direction («فعليك بتمثال الأشجار») is presented as fallback
permission, not disagreement. Hindiyya/Radd al-Muhtar visual chapters are
unextracted pending clip production — agreement/disagreement not asserted here.
Silence noted: no ruling on mixed animate+inanimate scenes or photographic media
(a pre-modern text).
