# Hadith Evidence

## [TEXT] Torn curtain → cushions: the degradation sequence (Muslim 2107i + Bukhari 5954)

- locators: https://sunnah.com/muslim:2107i · https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5954
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (base layer)
- local_evidence: row 1.7-cluster (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Whether a removed displayed image may be reused after tearing/cutting into
degraded (trodden) form.

### النص (excerpt)
«فَقَطَعْنَاهُ فَجَعَلْنَا مِنْهُ وِسَادَةً أَوْ وِسَادَتَيْنِ» — "We tore it into pieces
and made a cushion or two cushions out of that" (Muslim 2107i, after the
imitation-of-creation threat). «قَالَتْ فَجَعَلْنَاهُ وِسَادَةً أَوْ وِسَادَتَيْنِ» (5954).
Nasa'i's disjunct: head-cutting OR trodden rug — https://sunnah.com/nasai:5365.

### العلة (illah)
The source records the sequence (tearing → cutting → cushions); it does not name
the mechanism — extraction is descriptive. The Hanafi tier supplies the mechanism
words (imtihan/debasement) in knowledge/hanafi-visual.md.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
Tension with the pictured-cushion refusal (Bukhari 5957): whether cushion-reuse
reflects degradation-permission is recorded for REVIEW, not decided here.

### safe_zone hint
The recorded path from displayed image to licit reuse runs through tearing/cutting
into degraded form — no intact display survives in these narrations.
