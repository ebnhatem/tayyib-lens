## [TEXT] Jibril's curtain directive: cut off heads or make a trodden rug (nasai:5365)

- locator: https://sunnah.com/nasai:5365 (Sahih, Darussalam)
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (base layer)
- local_evidence: row 1.25 (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
A pictured household curtain blocking angelic entry — the two stated remediations.

### النص (excerpt)
«فَإِمَّا أَنْ تُقْطَعَ رُءُوسُهَا أَوْ تُجْعَلَ بِسَاطًا يُوطَأُ» — "You should either cut
off their heads or make it into a rug to be stepped on, for we Angels do not enter
a house in which there are images."

### العلة (illah)
Per the narration, the angels' bar («لاَ نَدْخُلُ بَيْتًا فِيهِ تَصَاوِيرُ»); the remedy
targets the image-form (heads) or the cloth's status (trodden rug).

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
Whether head-cutting alone suffices without degradation is not stated here —
silence feeds REVIEW. The doll-head pair (Islamweb 372403/173023) continues the
naqisa analysis in knowledge/ai-fatwas.md: the headless body is permitted by the
majority; the severed head alone is disputed (Shafi'i preponderant view prohibits;
Ibn Baz's face-distinction) — severed-head-alone outputs route to REVIEW.

### safe_zone hint
Per this narration, removing heads or converting to trodden use are the two recorded
exits — the spine of rule visual.incomplete_image.
## [TEXT] The taswir threat block: severest punishment for rivalling creation (Bukhari 5954/5955/5957, Muslim 2106a)

- locators: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5954 · https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5955 ·
  https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5957 · https://sunnah.com/muslim:2106a
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (base layer)
- local_evidence: rows 1.3–1.6 (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Displayed pictured furnishings (curtain, cushion) and the keeping of pictures —
the stated threat, the removal response, and the angels bar.

### النص (excerpt)
«أَشَدُّ النَّاسِ عَذَابًا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ الَّذِينَ يُضَاهُونَ بِخَلْقِ اللَّهِ» —
"the severest punishment … those who try to make the like of Allah's creations"
(5954; torn curtain → made into cushion(s)).
«وَعَلَّقْتُ دُرْنُوكًا فِيهِ تَمَاثِيلُ، فَأَمَرَنِي أَنْ أَنْزِعَهُ، فَنَزَعْتُهُ» —
removal on sight, no reuse recorded (5955).
«يُقَالُ لَهُمْ أَحْيُوا مَا خَلَقْتُمْ» + «الْمَلاَئِكَةُ لاَ تَدْخُلُ بَيْتًا فِيهِ الصُّورَةُ» —
the make-alive challenge and the angels bar (5957, pictured cushion refused).
«لاَ تَدْخُلُ الْمَلاَئِكَةُ بَيْتًا فِيهِ كَلْبٌ وَلاَ صُورَةٌ» (Muslim 2106a).

### العلة (illah)
Per the narrations: rivalry/imitation of creation (مضاهاة) and the ensoulment
challenge (إحياء); the 5955/2106a wordings state the order/bar without naming a
mechanism — silence is data.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
Tension recorded, not resolved: the pictured cushion refused entry (5957) against
the torn-curtain-to-cushion conversions (5954; Muslim 2107i); degradation scope
comes from companion narrations (nasai:5365 remediation), not from this block.
The Islamweb AI-image permissibility line (475069/519001) contests extension of
this block to device-dependent digital images — REVIEW-scoped.

### safe_zone hint
Per this block, intact displayed animate images are removed/barred; no continued
display is recorded — the spine of rule visual.full_body_animate.
## [TEXT] Ibn Abbas: trees and whatever has no soul (chain-pair Bukhari 2225 + Muslim 2110a)

- locator (Bukhari wording): https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2225
- locator (Muslim wording): https://sunnah.com/muslim:2110a
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (base layer)
- local_evidence: rows 1.2/1.14 pair (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
A professional image-maker asks Ibn Abbas for a verdict; Ibn Abbas transmits the
ensoulment-demand threat, then directs him to lifeless subjects if he insists.

### النص (excerpt)
«فَعَلَيْكَ بِهَذَا الشَّجَرِ، كُلِّ شَىْءٍ لَيْسَ فِيهِ رُوحٌ» — "I advise you to make
pictures of trees and any other unanimated objects" (Bukhari 2225).
«فَاصْنَعِ الشَّجَرَ وَمَا لاَ نَفْسَ لَهُ» — "paint the pictures of trees and lifeless
things" (Muslim 2110a, on unavoidability: «إِنْ كُنْتَ لاَ بُدَّ فَاعِلاً»).

### العلة (illah)
The threat turns on ensoulment («حَتَّى يَنْفُخَ فِيهَا الرُّوحَ» / a soul given to
every picture); what has no soul does not meet it.

### الشروط (conditions)
The lifeless-subject direction is conditional on insistence/unavoidability in both
wordings («إِنْ أَبَيْتَ إِلاَّ أَنْ تَصْنَعَ» / «إِنْ كُنْتَ لاَ بُدَّ فَاعِلاً»).

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
The general taswir threat block (Bukhari 5954/5955/5957, Muslim 2106a) stands
alongside this direction; the bounded dolls allowance (Bukhari 6130, pre-puberty
play only) is not generalized. Qurtubi's 34:13 abrogation claim is recorded in
knowledge/quran-evidence.md — REVIEW-scoped, not profile-backing here.

### safe_zone hint
Per this chain-pair, image-making confined to trees and "every thing in which there
is no soul" is the reported exit — the spine of rule visual.halal_base.
