# Hadith Evidence

## [TEXT] Aisha's dolls: bounded pre-puberty play allowance (Bukhari 6130)

- locator: https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6130
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (bounded exception)
- local_evidence: row 1.24 (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
Aisha's playing with dolls in the Prophet's presence — scope of the allowance.

### النص (excerpt)
«كُنْتُ أَلْعَبُ بِالْبَنَاتِ عِنْدَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم» — "I used to play with
the dolls in the presence of the Prophet." Page note: "(The playing with the dolls
and similar images is forbidden, but it was allowed for Aisha at that time, as she
was a little girl, not yet reached the age of puberty.) (Fath-ul-Bari page 143,
Vol.13)" — page-display text, not verified from Fath al-Bari itself.

### العلة (illah)
None stated for the allowance — the page grounds it in minority («a little girl,
not yet reached the age of puberty»), not in the dolls' form.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
The general threat block (5954/5957/2106a) stands alongside; the page's own note
bars generalization. The extraction lane's caution is adopted: bounded to young
children's play, NOT a general avatar license.
