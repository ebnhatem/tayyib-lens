## [TAFSIR] 21:52: Ibrahim's condemnation tied to worship, not mere form (Kathir + Tabari)

- locator: https://quran.com/21:52 (Kathir 21:51–56; Tabari transmitted reports)
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (scoping layer)
- local_evidence: row 1.18-series (verified 2026-10-23)

### المسألة (issue)
What exactly Ibrahim condemned in «مَا هَـٰذِهِ ٱلتَّمَاثِيلُ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَنتُمْ لَهَا عَـٰكِفُونَ» —
the form or the worship.

### النص (excerpt)
Kathir: "his denunciation of his people's worship of idols instead of Allah."
Tabari: «وكانت تلك التماثيل أصنامهم التي كانوا يعبدونها» (Mujahid report:
«قال: الأصنام»).

### العلة (illah)
Per both tafsirs, the stated connection is worship («يعبدونها» / "instead of Allah"),
not mere depiction. Qurtubi concurs on worship-constancy («مقيمون على عبادتها»)
while defining timthal broadly by resemblance — the breadth-vs-worship tension is
REVIEW material, recorded not resolved.

### الخلاف (disagreement — recorded, not resolved)
Qurtubi's 34:13 abrogation sentence («نسخ ذلك بشرع محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم»,
https://quran.com/34:13) is recorded as REVIEW-scoped shar'-man-qablana material;
it does not enter this rule's evidence. Silence noted: no tafsir surveyed maps
tamathil language onto machine generation.

### safe_zone hint
Per these tafsirs, condemnation attaches to worshipped objects — scoping support
for a lifeless-subject rule that claims no worship context.

## [TEXT] The pair, male and female — 4:1; 53:45–46; 92:3 (D2 binary anchors)

- locators: https://quran.com/4:1 · https://quran.com/53:45 · https://quran.com/92:3
- profile_binding: all visual profiles (depiction line)
- local_evidence: rows 1.39–1.40 (verified 2026-10-23)

### النص (excerpt)
