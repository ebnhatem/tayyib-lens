# knowledge/ — evidence cards

## What goes here
Curated evidence cards for: quran-evidence, hadith-evidence, hanafi-visual,
hanafi-audio, ai-fatwas. Each card cites its source with an exact locator.

## Rules
- Every card must carry one layer label at its heading:
  [TEXT] [TAFSIR] [MADHHAB] [FATWA] [EXTRAPOLATION] — never mix layers in one card.
- Arabic quotes: include the Arabic text (with basic diacritics) + translation + locator.
- Hadith cards must carry takhrij (collection, number, grade, sunnah.com locator) and
  note authentications/disagreements where they exist.
- Short excerpts only (a few lines); link the original. Full texts never enter this repo.
- Verify every locator opens and matches the claim before committing.
- Nothing here decides a verdict alone — cards feed policies/, they are not rules.

## Committing
`feat(knowledge): add <topic> evidence card (<source id>)` — locator in the body.
Definition of done: card labeled, locator verified, no full-text reproduction.
